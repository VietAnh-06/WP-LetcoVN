## 📚 Academic Programs & Visa Pathways

Daeduk University offers tailored programs designed for international students at every stage of their academic and professional journey:



### 1. D-4-1 (Korean Language Training Program)
- **Duration**: 6 months to 1 year (4 quarters per year: Spring, Summer, Autumn, Winter; 200 hours/quarter, total 800 hours/year) [1, 36, 37, 47, 215].
- **Schedule**: 5 days/week, 4 hours/day [1, 37]. Includes cultural experience trips every semester [36, 253, 293].
- **Tuition**: 4,900,000 KRW/year (4 semesters) or 2,500,000 KRW for 2 semesters [1, 39, 49, 381].
- **Dormitory (First 90 Days)**: 900,000 – 1,000,000 KRW (includes 2 meals/day — breakfast & dinner) [1, 39, 50, 381].
- **Admission Fee**: 100,000 KRW [1, 39, 50, 381].
- **Target Progression**: Reaching TOPIK Level 1 or 2 enables direct entry into the D-2-1 degree program [1, 47, 215].

### 2. D-2-1 (Associate Degree / Practical Vocational Track)
- **Duration**: 2 to 3 years (Degree completed in 2–3 years; option for 4-year Bachelor's degree link) [5, 21, 58].
- **Model**: Features 80% onsite enterprise training with 15 credits/semester recognized [149, 198, 212, 310].
- **Scholarships**: Automatic **40% tuition reduction** for the first semester [6, 24, 248, 360, 394].
- **Target Visa Output**: Post-graduation transition to **E-7-1 Professional Worker Visa** with guaranteed corporate hiring [4, 48, 69, 145, 210, 215].

### 3. D-2-6 (International Exchange Student Track - K-Tech Bridge)
- **Eligibility**: Enrolled students at international partner universities (e.g., senior students in engineering/business) [27, 253, 317, 319].
- **Duration**: 6 months or 1 year [27, 253].
- **Tuition Rate**:
  - Non-TOPIK 2 Holders: 1,450,000 KRW/semester [31, 257, 293].
  - TOPIK 2+ Holders: **600,000 KRW/semester** (Discounted tuition) [32, 257, 294].
- **Dormitory (Mandatory 90 Days)**: ~1,200,000 KRW/semester (includes 2 meals/day) [31, 32, 71, 257, 293].

## 📋 Admission Requirements & Application Roadmap

### 1. Eligibility Criteria

- **Academic Qualifications**: High school graduate (THPT) or equivalent [1, 21, 46, 59].
- **GPA**: Minimum **6.5 / 10.0** over 3 years of high school [1, 46, 59].
- **Age**: 18 to 25 years old [1, 46].
- **Gap Year**: Maximum **3 years** since high school graduation [1, 46].
- **Attendance**: Fewer than **10 unexcused absences** during 3 years of high school [1, 46].
- **Language Level**:
  - D-4-1 Language Program: No prior Korean language required [1, 47, 215].
  - D-2-1 Degree Program: **TOPIK Level 2 or higher** (or passing Daeduk University's online/offline placement test) [21, 23, 59, 392].
- **Financial Status**: Clean background and immigration compliance record [1, 46, 47].
